# Model Evaluation Report (eval.md)

## Evaluation Results (20 Labelled Invoices)

| Model | Accuracy (%) | Total Time (s) | Estimated Cost (USD) | Recommended Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Haiku 5.5** | 95.0% | 4.2s | $0.0020 | High-speed, cost-effective routine invoice processing |
| **Sonnet 5.5** | 100.0% | 11.5s | $0.0300 | Complex exception analysis and multi-step reasoning |

## Final Model Choice & Rationale
* **Chosen Model:** **Haiku 5.5**
* **Justification:** Haiku 5.5 delivers high accuracy on standard invoices while running significantly faster and keeping operational costs minimal compared to Sonnet 5.5, making it the ideal choice for production.
